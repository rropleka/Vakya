# Sanskrit → Prakrit (the dramatic Prakrits, ca. 200 CE)
#
# Source: Wikipedia, "Prakrit", section "Development of Prakrit from Old Indo-Aryan", subsections "Early
# changes common to all Indo-Aryan languages", "Sound changes common to Pali and Prakrit", "Changes after
# the split of Pali and Prakrit" and "Changes up to Dramatic Prakrits" (retrieved 2026-11-07)
#   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakrit
#
# In the page's order. Changes it calls sporadic or unpredictable (rhotic cerebralisation, the a/u
# outcomes of ṛ) and the cluster assimilations, which this notation can't write generally, are left out.

from: Sanskrit
to: Prakrit

class C = [type=Consonant]
class V = [type=Vowel]

Loss of accent: the Vedic pitch accent is lost.
    V[+stressed] → V[-stressed]

Monophthongisation: before a consonant, e (aj) and o (aw) become ē and ō.
    aj aw → eː oː / _C, _#

Final consonants: final plosives are lost, lengthening the vowel; final -aḥ becomes -o and other final
visarga is lost; final nasals nasalise the vowel.
    Vk Vʈ Vt Vp → V[+long] V[+long] V[+long] V[+long] / _#
    ah → oː / _#
    h → ∅ / _#
    Vm Vn → V[+nasal] V[+nasal] / _#

MIA monophthongisation: aj, aja, aji, ajō, avi become ē; aw, ava become ō.
    {aj, aja, aji, ajoː, aʋi} → eː
    {aw, aʋa} → oː

Loss of ṛ (Central dialect): initial ṛ becomes ri, medial ṛ becomes i.
# The page: "cases of ṛ > a and ṛ > u, especially after labial, are occasionally found".
    r̩ → ri / #_
    r̩ → i

Sibilants: ś, ṣ and s merge as s.
    ɕ ʂ → s s

Shortening: a long vowel before a consonant cluster is shortened.
    V[+long] → V[-long] / _CC

Initial y: initial y becomes j.
    j → dʒ / #_

Nasals: n and ṇ merge as ṇ.
    n → ɳ

Lenition of intervocalic stops: single voiceless stops become voiced; the non-retroflex ones then
become spirants; aspirated spirants become h, β becomes v, and the remaining spirants are lost.
    p t k tʃ → b d g dʒ / V_V
    pʰ tʰ kʰ → bʱ dʱ gʱ / V_V
    g gʱ dʒ d dʱ b bʱ → ɣ ɣʱ ʑ ð ðʱ β βʱ / V_V
    ɣʱ ðʱ βʱ → ɦ ɦ ɦ
    β → ʋ
    ɣ ʑ ð → ∅ ∅ ∅

Lenition of y: intervocalic y is lost.
    j → ∅ / V_V
