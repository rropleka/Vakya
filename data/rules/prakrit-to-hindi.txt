# Prakrit → Hindi
#
# Source: Wikipedia, "Prakrit", section "Up to Apabhramsha", the Late Prakrit stage that Hindi continues
# (retrieved 2026-11-07)
#   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prakrit
#
# NOTE: only the Apabhramsha changes on that page; the New Indo-Aryan changes from Apabhramsha to Hindi
# (schwa deletion and others) aren't modelled yet.

from: Prakrit
to: Hindi

class V = [type=Vowel]

Final long vowels: final long vowels shorten, merging with the short vowels (ā ī ū ē ō become a i u i u).
    aː iː uː eː oː → a i u i u / _#

Intervocalic v: v is lost after a high vowel.
    ʋ → ∅ / {i, u}_V

Shortening of ū: long ū before a vowel is shortened.
    uː → u / _V
