# Proto-Indo-European → Proto-Indo-Iranian
#
# Source: Wikipedia, "Proto-Indo-Iranian language", section "Historical phonology" (retrieved 2026-11-07)
#   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Indo-Iranian_language
#
# The page gives "a fuller list of some of the hypothesized sound changes" without claiming an order,
# except that laryngeal vocalisation preceded the second palatalisation and that the laryngeal merger was
# about contemporary with the e/o merger. The order here is otherwise a choice (NOTE: lines).
#
# PII palatals ć ȷ́ ȷ́ʰ are written c ɟ ɟʱ, the second palatal series č ǰ ǰʰ as tʃ dʒ dʒʱ, and the merged
# laryngeal *H as the glottal stop ʔ (the page: "which may have been a glottal stop").

from: Proto-Indo-European
to: Proto-Indo-Iranian

class C = [type=Consonant]
class V = [type=Vowel]
class H = {h₁, h₂, h₃}

Satem shift: the PIE palatals become PII ć ȷ́ ȷ́ʰ; the labiovelars merge with the plain velars.
    kʲ gʲ gʲʱ → c ɟ ɟʱ
    kʷ gʷ gʷʱ → k g gʱ

Laryngeal aspiration: h₂ aspirates a preceding stop; a laryngeal before a vowel disappears.
# NOTE: "and possibly h₁" is left out.
    p t k c → pʰ tʰ kʰ cʰ / _h₂V
    H → ∅ / _V

Brugmann's law: o in an open syllable lengthens to ā.
    o → aː / _CV

Bartholomae's law: an aspirate followed by a voiceless consonant becomes a voiced stop plus a voiced
aspirate.
# NOTE: written for t only (dʰt > ddʰ); the page also gives s.
    bʱt dʱt gʱt → bdʱ ddʱ gdʱ

Ruki: s becomes š after a liquid, a high vowel or a velar.
# NOTE: the velars are listed in their post-satem form; "the syllabic laryngeal" is left out.
    s → ʂ / {r, r̩, l, l̩, i, iː, u, uː, k, g, gʱ, c, ɟ, ɟʱ}_

Liquids: l, r, l̥, r̥ merge as r and r̥.
    l l̩ → r r̩

Syllabic nasals: m̥ and n̥ merge with a; word-final m̥ becomes am.
    m̩ → am / _#
    m̩ n̩ → a a

Final r: word-final r is lost after long vowels.
    r → ∅ / V[+long]_#

Laryngeal vocalisation: a laryngeal becomes i after a consonant and before a consonant cluster, or after a
consonant at the end of a word.
    H → i / C_CC, C_#

Second palatalisation (law of palatals): k, g, gʰ become č, ǰ, ǰʰ before the front vowels i, e.
    k g gʱ → tʃ dʒ dʒʱ / _{i, iː, e, eː}

Merger of e and o with a: e, o merge with a, and ē, ō with ā.
    e o eː oː → a a aː aː

Merger of laryngeals: the remaining laryngeals merge as *H.
    h₁ h₂ h₃ → ʔ ʔ ʔ

Lubotsky's law: H disappears before a voiced unaspirated stop followed by another consonant.
    ʔ → ∅ / _{b, d, g, ɟ, dʒ}C
