# Proto-Indo-Iranian → Sanskrit (Vedic)
#
# Source: Wikipedia, "Proto-Indo-Iranian language", section "Subsequent sound changes" (its table of
# reflexes, Sanskrit column: regular outcome and conditioned outcomes) and section "Two palatal series";
# retrieved 2026-11-07.
#   https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proto-Indo-Iranian_language
#
# The merged laryngeal *H is written ʔ (see pie-to-proto-indo-iranian.txt). The table gives outcomes,
# not an order; the order here is a choice (NOTE: lines). Not modelled: the loss of the voiced sibilants
# *z, *ẓ, *ź / *ž, *žʰ with lengthening, the cluster outcomes (kṣ, ch, cch, jj), and retroflexion of dentals
# after *š.

from: Proto-Indo-Iranian
to: Sanskrit

class C = [type=Consonant]
class V = [type=Vowel]
class L = {p, pʰ, b, bʱ, m, w}
class A = {pʰ, tʰ, kʰ, cʰ, bʱ, dʱ, gʱ, ɟʱ, dʒʱ}

Syllabic r before H: *r̥H becomes īr, or ūr next to labials.
    r̩ʔ → uːr / L_
    r̩ʔ → iːr

Laryngeal: *H becomes i between consonants; elsewhere it is lost, lengthening a preceding vowel.
    ʔ → i / C_C
    Vʔ → V[+long]
    ʔ → ∅

Grassmann's law: an aspirate loses its aspiration when another aspirate follows in the next syllable.
# NOTE: the table lists Grassmann outcomes (ph > p, ȷ́ʰ > j, ...) but not its exact conditions; this is
# the standard statement. It precedes the palatal changes, since ȷ́ʰ > j by Grassmann (not h).
    pʰ tʰ kʰ bʱ dʱ gʱ ɟʱ dʒʱ → p t k b d g ɟ dʒ / _V(C)A, _VC(C)A

Palatal series: the first series ć ȷ́ ȷ́ʰ becomes Sanskrit ś j h; of the second series č ǰ ǰʰ, ǰʰ becomes h
(č and ǰ stay, as Sanskrit c and j).
    c ɟ ɟʱ dʒʱ → ɕ dʒ ɦ ɦ

Semivowel w: *w becomes v, except after a vowel, where it is the u of a diphthong (au, o).
    w → ʋ // V_C, V_#

Visarga: *s, *š and *r become ḥ before a pause (word-finally here).
    s ʂ r → h h h / _#

Retroflex n: *n becomes ṇ after a retroflex consonant or *r within the same word, with no palatal or
dental consonant in between.
# NOTE: "in between" is approximated as at most one intervening vowel and one non-dental consonant.
    n → ɳ / {r, r̩, ʂ}_, {r, r̩, ʂ}V_, {r, r̩, ʂ}V{k, g, p, b, m, w, j, ɦ}V_
