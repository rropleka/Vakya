pirantization: Aspirated stops became fricatives.

    Phonological rule: pʰ tʰ kʰ → f θ x

Spirantization: Stops became fricatives before another consonants. Exceptions: *tt became *st, while *pt retained in Avestan.

    Phonological rule: p t k → f θ x / _C

Deaspiration: All voiced aspirated plosives became simple plosives.

    Phonological rule: C[+aspiration] → C[-aspiration]

Debuccalization: s became h when not before plosives or n.

    Phonological rule: C[+aspiration] → C[-aspiration]s → h / × _{P, n}

Assibilation: Dentals become sibilants preceding another dentals. *t → *s and *d → *z

    Phonological rule: C[+dental] → C[+sibilant] / _C[+dental]
